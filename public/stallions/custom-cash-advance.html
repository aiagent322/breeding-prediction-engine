Full Profile

Custom Cash Advance -- 2009 Stallion. AQHA. 15H. By Custom Crome (NRHA Three Million Dollar Sire; NRHA #6 All-Time Leading Sire; Top 10 NRHA Leading Sire multiple years; Custom Crome's sons and daughters have dominated open and non-pro reining competition at the NRHA Futurity and Derby level across multiple generations; the Custom Crome line is distinct from the dominant Colonels Smoking Gun and Topsail Whiz sire lines that dominate most Silver Spurs stallions, making Custom Cash Advance a genuine pedigree outcross for breeders whose mares already carry heavy Gunner or Whiz blood) out of Cash In Roan (Topsail Cody x -- Topsail Cody is the NRHA Million Dollar Sire and Hall of Fame inductee whose son Topsail Whiz became the NRHA $12 Million Sire; Cash In Roan is listed by Silver Spurs as a Leading NRHA Producer; the Topsail Cody foundation on the dam side connects Custom Cash Advance directly to the Topsail family tree through the most foundational link available -- Topsail Cody himself rather than through the more commonly seen Topsail Whiz sons).

Owned by Silver Spurs Equine LLC. Scottsdale, Arizona. (480) 563-1966. Cooled and frozen semen available.

Stud fee $3,000. Silver Spurs Breeders Futurity enrolled. Source: https://www.silverspursequine.com/custom-cash-advance-1 LTE $228,608 confirmed NRHA. Confirmed career: 2013 NRBC 7UP Open Challenge Champion; 2011 NRHA Open Derby 3rd; 2012 NRHA Open Derby 3rd; 2012 NRBC Open Derby 3rd; 2012 NRHA Shootout split 4th; 2011 NRBC Open Derby 3rd; 2010 NRBC Open Derby Top 10; 2010 AQHA World Show Junior Reining 4th; 2011 Cactus Reining Classic Open Derby 3rd; 2011 Cowboy Capital Classic Open Derby split 3rd; 2011 High Roller Classic Open Derby split 5th.

Ranch riding offspring confirmed via AQHA 2024 Ranch Horse Sire rankings. Placing 3rd at the NRHA Open Derby in both 2011 and 2012 is a sustained consecutive performance matched by very few horses in the sport's history. The NRHA Derby attracts the best four, five, and six-year-old reining horses from the world's most accomplished open professionals, and a top-three finish in the Open division in two consecutive years confirms that Custom Cash Advance's talent was not a single-season peak. Adding the 2013 NRBC 7UP Open Challenge Championship further establishes that his competitive ability carried across multiple seasons and multiple premier events.

His Custom Crome sire line is the most commercially underrepresented high-production sire line in the Silver Spurs stallion roster. While the roster is dominated by Colonels Smoking Gun sons, Topsail Whiz sons, and Shining Spark sons, Custom Crome through Custom Cash Advance provides an elite NRHA Three Million Dollar Sire cross that crosses well with all the dominant dam-line genetics the other Silver Spurs stallions have produced in their offspring. Breeders whose mares carry Gunner, Wimpys Little Step, or Shining Spark blood will find Custom Cash Advance a complementary cross that brings Custom Crome genetics without concentration risk. Offspring confirmed competing in AQHA ranch riding as well as NRHA reining confirm the cross-discipline versatility the Custom Crome x Topsail Cody dam combination produces.
