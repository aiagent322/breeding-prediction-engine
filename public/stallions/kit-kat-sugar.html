About Kit Kat Sugar
Kit Kat Sugar — 2008 Sorrel Stallion, AQHA, owned by XIT Ranch, LLC. Standing at the historic Four Sixes Ranch, Granbury TX. Bred by Barbara and Lonnie Allsup. Full information and booking at 6666stallions.com and xitranch.com.
By High Brow Cat (LTE $110,800 · NCHA #1 All-Time Leading Sire · $95M+ offspring · sire of the earners of more than $95 million — the most productive sire in NCHA history) out of Sugar N Dulce (Smart Lil Ricochet × Little Badger Dulce — NCHA Open Classic Challenge finalist; second dam Little Badger Dulce earned $668,461 as NCHA Horse of the Year, NCHA Open Super Stakes Champion, NCHA Open Futurity Reserve Champion, NCHA Non-Pro World Champion, and NCHA Open Reserve World Champion twice). The pedigree combines two of the deepest cow horse production lines in the breed: High Brow Cat's surgical precision on cattle and Little Badger Dulce's championship mare record through the dam side.
"Kit Kat Sugar is not comparable to any other stallion. He checks all the boxes: great feet, conformation, an intelligent mind, and exceptional athleticism."
— Ben Lard, XIT Ranch Manager
Skeet dominated his 4-year-old season with six major event championship wins — Bonanza, Brazos Bash, Cattlemens, El Rancho, Wrangler, and Idaho CHA Open Derby Reserve — before earning the 2012 NCHA Open Horse of the Year title and the NCHA Super Stakes Reserve Championship, then retiring sound. As a sire, his ascent has been steep: NCHA #7 Leading Cutting Sire in 2021, QData Top 10 Cutting and #6 Reined Cow Horse Sire in 2023, and #1 Tie-Down Roping Sire in 2023 — a cross-discipline production record matched by very few stallions in the industry. The 2023 NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity gave him NRCHA Million Dollar Sire status. Total offspring earnings now exceed $14 million. His son Scooter Kat holds the record as NRCHA's all-time leading money earner.
