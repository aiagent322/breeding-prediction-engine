Full Profile

A Sparkling Vintage -- 1997 Bay Stallion. AQHA. 14.3H. ICSI only.

By Shining Spark (Genuine Doc x Diamonds Sparkle by Mr Diamond Dude -- NRHA Hall of Fame; LTE $62,674; AQHA Junior Reining World Champion 1993; NRHA Derby Open Champion 1994; Equi-Stat All-Time Leading Reined Cow Horse Sire; top-10 All-Time Leading Reining Sire; PE $10.3M+ in NRHA alone; producers with get earnings in excess of $16M; sire of Jacs Electric Spark osw022, Shiners Voodoo Dr ss026, and many other Silver Spurs and Oswood stallions in this database -- Shining Spark's sons and daughters have dominated reining and reined cow horse competition across every level of competition from futurity through aged events for three consecutive decades) out of Vintage Badger (Peppy San Badger x -- Peppy San Badger is the NRHA Hall of Fame inductee and the foundation of the Peptoboonsmal/Peppy San Badger cow sense line that defines modern NCHA and NRCHA competition; Vintage Badger's produce record confirms A Sparkling Vintage as her standout performer and one of the most commercially successful breeding stallions of the 2000-2020 era; the Shining Spark x Peppy San Badger dam cross in A Sparkling Vintage pairing two foundational Hall of Fame sire lines in a single individual). Owned by Petra Zeitler Conner and Silver Spurs Equine LLC. Silver Spurs Equine LLC, 14445 East Quail Track Road, Scottsdale AZ 85262. (480) 563-1966.

ICSI only -- live foal payment policy: pay breeding fee only when you have a live foal. Stud fee $6,000. Silver Spurs Breeders Futurity enrolled. Source: https://www.silverspursequine.com/a-sparkling-vintage LTE $82,516 total confirmed (NRHA $54,516 + European earnings $28,000).

PE $4,654,000+ confirmed Silver Spurs Equine. NRHA $4 Million Sire. Sire of two NRHA Triple Crown Champions -- the only stallion at Silver Spurs to produce two horses that each won the NRHA Triple Crown (NRHA Futurity, NRBC, and NRHA Derby championships in a single career). Confirmed career: 2001 NRBC Level 3 Open Champion under Ruben Vandorp; top 10 NRHA Open Futurity; 3rd NRHA Intermediate Open Derby; top 10 NRHA Open Derby; ANR Maturity Open & Limited Open Champion; 4th NRHA Intermediate Open Futurity; split 7th NRBC Intermediate Open Derby; NRBC Derby Intermediate Open Champion.

Top offspring include Platinum Vintage (LTE $390,828; 2015 NRHA Derby Open Champion; 2016 Cactus Classic Open Derby Champion; Andrea Fappani's choice for the 2019 and 2021 Run For A Million); A Vintage Smoke (LTE $612,288; NRHA Triple Crown Champion; 2020 NRHA Derby Level 4 Open Champion; NRHA #1 All-Time Black horse earner); All American Vintage (LTE $171,263; 2017 NRHA Open Futurity Reserve Champion; 2017 Congress Open Futurity Champion; 2017 Tulsa Reining Classic Open Futurity Champion); Starjac Vintage (LTE $228,312; 2022 High Roller Reining Classic L4 Open Derby Champion; NRHA Futurity, Derby and NRBC L4 Open Finalist); SS Pretty Nifty (LTE $174,845; 2024 100X Euro Classic Reining Derby L4 Non-Pro Champion). The production of two NRHA Triple Crown Champions from a single sire is an elite accomplishment. In NRHA history, only a handful of stallions have ever produced a Triple Crown Champion at all. Producing two -- Platinum Vintage and A Vintage Smoke -- from the same sire confirms that A Sparkling Vintage consistently produces horses with the talent, trainability, and competitive consistency required to win across all three of the premier NRHA annual events in a single career.

The Shining Spark x Peppy San Badger dam cross through Vintage Badger creates offspring with both the natural lope and stop precision inherited from the Shining Spark sire line and the cow sense, trainability, and competitive intelligence that Peppy San Badger has transmitted through every generation of his descendants. Breeders crossing Topsail Whiz daughters, Wimpys Little Step daughters, or Gunnatrashya daughters on A Sparkling Vintage access a proven $4.65M PE cross at Silver Spurs' ICSI-only program with the added commercial security of a live foal payment policy.
