Full Profile

Chic Dreamin -- 2012 Sorrel Stallion. AQHA. 14.3H. 5-panel N/N, IMM N/N.

"Skeeter." By Magnum Chic Dream (Smart Chic Olena x Sailin Barbee by Topsail Cody -- NRHA Hall of Fame; NRHA $13 Million Sire -- one of only five stallions in NRHA history to reach the $13 million mark; nearly 900 NRHA money-earning offspring; 30 offspring with $100,000+ NRHA LTE; 2004 AQHA Senior Reining World Champion; sired by Smart Chic Olena whose PE exceeds $14M across cutting, reining and cow horse; Chic Dreamin is confirmed as Magnum Chic Dream's 4th highest-earning offspring by NRHA LTE at $250,421) out of Skeets Red Dunit (Skeets Peppy x Brennas Red Dunit -- Skeets Peppy LTE $85,306; Equi-Stat Elite $1 Million Sire; NRHA Open Futurity finalist; NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity finalist; 2016 NRCHA World's Greatest Horseman winner Skeets Oak Peppy is by Skeets Peppy; Skeets Peppy is by Peppy San Badger out of Tivios Miss Skeet by Right On Tivio; Brennas Red Dunit is by Hollywood Dun It out of Be Aechs Brenna by Be Aech Enterprise -- a pedigree that carries the Be Aech Enterprise Hall of Fame line and Hollywood Dun It Hall of Fame foundation through the dam side; Skeets Red Dunit is an unshown mare who is Silver Spurs' own champion producer). Bred and nominated by Casey and Kathy Hinton. Owned by Silver Spurs Equine LLC. Scottsdale, Arizona.

Cooled and frozen semen available. Stud fee $2,000 ($500 booking fee, balance on confirmed pregnancy). Silver Spurs Breeders Futurity enrolled. Enrolled NRBC, NRHA Sire & Dam, NRHA Germany, IRHBA.

Source: https://www.silverspursequine.com/chicdreamin LTE $265,420 confirmed NRHA. PE $250,421 confirmed NRHA (Magnum Chic Dream sire record confirmed). NRBC Open Champion. AQHA World Champion Junior Reining.

Confirmed career: 2018 Reining By The Bay Open Derby Champion (score 232); 2018 NRHA Open Derby 4th; 2017 AQHA World Champion Junior Reining; 2017 NRHA Open Derby 4th; 2017 NRBC Open Derby Champion (score 229); 2016 NRBC Intermediate Open Derby Champion. Silver Spurs ranch manager Brett Stone described Chic Dreamin's career as one defined by consistently elite performances across multiple premier events across multiple seasons. Winning both the 2017 NRBC Open Derby and the 2018 Reining By The Bay Open Derby Championship -- separated by roughly six months -- and scoring 229 and 232 respectively demonstrates that Chic Dreamin was among the most consistent open performers in professional reining across his entire aged-event career. The 2017 AQHA World Champion Junior Reining title alongside the NRBC Open Derby Championship in the same year confirms he competed and won at both the premier open professional event level and the AQHA world championship show simultaneously -- a very rare dual accomplishment.

As the 4th highest-earning son of Magnum Chic Dream at $250,421 NRHA LTE, Chic Dreamin carries one of the most commercially significant sire pedigrees available in the Silver Spurs roster. Magnum Chic Dream's $13M PE and 900 money-earners confirm that his progeny consistently win across NRHA open, non-pro and youth divisions worldwide. Breeders crossing Colonels Smoking Gun daughters or Wimpys Little Step daughters on Chic Dreamin access the Magnum Chic Dream x Skeets Peppy dam cross at Silver Spurs' $2,000 stud fee -- representing exceptional value relative to the pedigree depth and personal performance credentials at one of the sport's most commercially active breeding stations.
