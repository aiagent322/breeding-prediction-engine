Full Profile

Wimpys Little Step -- 1999 Palomino Stallion. AQHA. 14.3H. ICSI only.

By Nu Chex To Cash (Nu Chex x Candy Chex by Poco Enterprise -- NRHA Three Million Dollar Sire; LTE $185,757; 2000 NRHA Non-Pro Futurity Champion; NRHA Intermediate Open World Champion; NRHA Limited Open World Champion; Supreme Reining Horse; sire also of Big Chex To Cash ss002, Lil Joe Cash osw026 and multiple other champions in this database; the Nu Chex line carries the King foundation genetics through Nu Chex that paired exceptionally with the Leolita Step dam side to produce the most commercially successful individual reining sire in NRHA history) out of Leolita Step (Forty Seven x -- Forty Seven is a foundation AQHA performance stallion; Leolita Step is an NRHA producer whose son Wimpys Little Step confirmed her status as one of the most significant broodmares in modern reining history by producing the sport's #1 All-Time Living Sire during his peak years; the Nu Chex To Cash x Leolita Step cross is unique in that it produced an individual who eclipsed both his sire (Nu Chex To Cash at $3M PE) and his sire's all-time competitors to become the leading NRHA sire of his era). Owned by Silver Spurs Equine / Xtra Quarter Horses LLC, Purcell OK. ICSI only. Stud fee $6,500 (balance due only after live foal is confirmed on ground).

Silver Spurs Breeders Futurity enrolled. Source: https://www.silverspursequine.com/wimpys-little-step LTE $185,757 confirmed NRHA. PE $15,165,000+ confirmed Silver Spurs Equine -- NRHA $15 Million Sire. The #1 NRHA All-Time Living Sire during his peak years; #1 or #2 Leading Sire of Reining Money-Earners for 8 out of 10 years his foals showed.

The first NRHA Open Futurity Champion to sire an NRHA Open Futurity Champion in his first foal crop. 1,988+ Wimpy foals on the ground with 50% money-earning rate and average earnings of $11,000+ per performer. Confirmed career: 2002 NRHA Open Futurity Champion (score 233 ridden by Shawn Flarida -- a standing ovation run); All American Quarter Horse Congress Futurity Champion; Tradition Open Reining Futurity Reserve Champion. His 2002 NRHA Open Futurity Championship run was historic.

Ridden by NRHA Hall of Famer and Five Million Dollar Rider Shawn Flarida, Wimpys Little Step scored a 233 at the Futurity -- a near-perfect score that earned a standing ovation from the crowd and established the palomino as one of the most celebrated Futurity Champions in NRHA history. The NRHA Hall of Fame citation recognized Wimpy as the first Futurity Champion sire whose first foal crop immediately produced another Futurity Champion -- an achievement without precedent in NRHA breeding history. Top offspring include Wimpys Little Chic (LTE $516,556; NRHA Open Futurity Champion; NRHA Open Derby Champion; NRBC Champion; Triple Crown; NRHA Hall of Fame; #1 All-Time NRHA Money-Earner); RC Fancy Step (LTE $364,937; NRHA Open Derby Champion; NRBC Reserve Champion; #5 All-Time NRHA Money-Earner); Wimpys Little Buddy (LTE $285,358; 5th NRHA Open Futurity; AQHA World Senior Reining); Wimpyneedsacocktail (LTE $281,804; 2013 NRBC Open Champion -- Silver Spurs' own stallion); SHESOUTTAYOURLEAGUE (LTE $185,764; 2015 NRHA Open Futurity Champion x Walla Walla Whiz osw002's mate). With 50% of 1,988+ foals earning money in the show pen, Wimpys Little Step is the most productive reining stallion in history measured by offspring money-earning consistency.

His ICSI at $6,500 with live foal payment policy gives breeders access to NRHA's all-time benchmark sire at Silver Spurs Equine Oklahoma.
