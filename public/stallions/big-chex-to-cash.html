By Nu Chex To Cash (Nu Chex x -- 1999 AQHA Palomino stallion; NRHA LTE $185,757; NRHA Three Million Dollar Sire; 2000 NRHA Non-Pro Futurity Champion; NRHA Intermediate Open World Champion; NRHA Limited Open World Champion; leading sire whose sons and daughters dominate reining and ranch riding; ss001 connection: Nu Chex To Cash also sired Lil Joe Cash osw026 in this database confirming the breadth of his sire influence across the western performance horse world; nu Chex is by the legendary King foundation sire with deep Quarter Horse heritage) out of Snip O Gun (One Gun x -- NRHA Hall of Fame inductee; dam of Big Chex To Cash as her career-defining produce; One Gun is a son of Gunner -- Colonels Smoking Gun, the NRHA Hall of Fame $15 Million Sire who also sired Gunnatrashya and Down Right Amazing in this database; the One Gun x Nu Chex To Cash pedigree cross in Big Chex To Cash places two major reining sire lines in a single individual through his dam's Hall of Fame credentials; Snip O Gun's Hall of Fame induction confirmed December 2019). NRHA Million Dollar Sire. Confirmed career highlights: 2005 NRHA Open Futurity Reserve Champion ridden by Andrea Fappani; 2005 West Coast Spectacular Open Reining Futurity Champion; 2007 NRHA Open Derby Reserve Champion ridden by Andrea Fappani; 2007 NRBC Open Derby Finalist; 2007 Reining By The Bay Derby Co-Champion; 2007 Arizona Sun Circuit High-Point Junior Reining Open. Earning Reserve Champion titles at both the NRHA Open Futurity (2005) and the NRHA Open Derby (2007) under NRHA Five Million Dollar Rider Andrea Fappani confirms elite competitive ability across multiple seasons and multiple premier events. The NRHA Futurity and NRHA Derby are the two most prestigious annual NRHA events, and placing second at the Open level in both -- two years apart -- demonstrates the consistency and durability that defines truly exceptional individuals rather than one-year wonders.

His top offspring include Cashing Black Chex (LTE $172,895), Big Creme Chic (LTE $88,102), Checksworthy (LTE $67,312), Big Chex Nic (LTE $70,236 -- 1st Congress Reining Futurity L3 Open), and Cold Hard Smart Cash (LTE $62,907 -- 3rd NRCHA Futurity Amateur, 2nd NRCHA World Bridle Intermediate Non-Pro). A total of 473 AQHA foals were registered, with 163 NRHA money-earners and 66 AQHA point-earners. His offspring compete across the full spectrum of reining, ranch riding, NRCHA, and European reining circuits. The Nu Chex To Cash x Snip O Gun cross united one of the top living reining sires of his era with a Hall of Fame producing dam whose One Gun by Gunner foundation brought competitive fire, natural athleticism, and the signature stop of the Colonels Smoking Gun line.
