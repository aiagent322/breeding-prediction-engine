Full Profile

Spooks Gotta Gun -- 2002 Bay Overo Stallion. AQHA & APHA double registered. 14.3H. 5-panel N/N, LWO N/N, IMM N/N.

In Memoriam 2002–2023. By Grays Starlight (Peppy San Badger x Doc's Starlight by Doc Bar -- deceased October 2002; NRHA Million Dollar Sire in both Reining and Cutting simultaneously -- the only stallion in history to surpass the Million Dollar Sire mark in both disciplines; total offspring earnings over $10 million; sire of Gunner through Katie Gun who is also Spooks Gotta Gun's dam; Like A Diamond osw024 in this database is also by Grays Starlight through a different dam, making Spooks Gotta Gun a direct half-sibling in blood to Like A Diamond through the Grays Starlight sire) out of Katie Gun (John Gun x -- NRHA Top Ten Leading Producer; AQHA High Point Junior Reining Champion; High Point Reining Mare; Top-Ten AQHA World Championship Team Penning; Superior Reining; Amateur ROM; most importantly the dam of NRHA Hall of Fame stallion Gunner -- Colonels Smoking Gun -- whose PE exceeds $15 million and who sired Gunnatrashya, Down Right Amazing, and multiple other champions in this database; Spooks Gotta Gun is a full brother in blood to Gunner through the same sire and dam, making the Grays Starlight x Katie Gun cross one of the most historically significant breeding combinations in NRHA history). Bred by Eric Storey. Owned by Silver Spurs Equine LLC.

Frozen ICSI semen. Stud fee $6,500 (balance due after live foal). Silver Spurs Breeders Futurity enrolled. Source: https://www.silverspursequine.com/spooks-gotta-gun LTE $81,637 confirmed NRHA.

PE $4,101,569 confirmed Silver Spurs Equine. NRHA Three Million Dollar Sire -- confirmed the week of his death November 2023. Confirmed career: 2007 APHA World Champion Junior Reining Horse; 2008 NRHA Derby Level 4 Open 3rd; 2008 Reining By The Bay Open Derby Champion; 2009 AQHA World Senior Reining 3rd; APHA Open ROM. NRHA Triple Crown Champion Sire -- first foal crop produced NRHA Futurity Champion, NRHA Derby Champion, and NRBC Champion.

RGP #1 All-Time Leading Sire of Ranch Riding Money-Earners. AQHA Top 5 All-Time Leading Sire of Ranch Riding Point-Earners. Being a full brother in blood to Gunner through the Grays Starlight x Katie Gun cross is the most commercially significant aspect of Spooks Gotta Gun's pedigree. Gunner -- Colonels Smoking Gun -- is the NRHA Hall of Fame stallion and $15 Million Sire whose offspring include Gunnatrashya osw001 ($15M PE), Down Right Amazing osw013 (first $1M earner in NRHA history), Tinker With Guns ss011, and dozens of other champions in this database.

Spooks Gotta Gun and Gunner share identical sire and dam, giving Spooks offspring the full genetic foundation of the most successful NRHA sire family of the modern era from both parents simultaneously. Top offspring include Spooks Gotta Whiz (LTE $346,715; NRHA Open Futurity Champion; NRHA Derby Champion; NRBC Champion; WEG Gold Medalist; NRHA Hall of Fame -- osw003 in this database); Golden Coins (LTE $235,206; NRBC Derby L4 Open Champion; NRHA Derby L4 Open Reserve Champion; Run For A Million Champion); Spooks Gotta Tinsel (LTE $170,685; Run For A Million finalist); SpooksGottaChicoLena (LTE $155,106); Oops Itsa Spooks (LTE $125,559); Gotta Hot Gun (LTE $119,652; AQHA World JR Ranch Riding Open Champion). His frozen ICSI semen at $6,500 with live foal payment policy gives breeders access to the Grays Starlight x Katie Gun cross -- the same combination that produced both Gunner and Spooks Gotta Whiz -- at Silver Spurs Equine.
