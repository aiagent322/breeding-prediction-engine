Full Profile

Conquistador Whiz -- 1995 Bay Stallion. AQHA & APHA. 14.3H. 5-panel N/N.

By Topsail Whiz (Topsail Cody x Jeanie Whiz Bar by Cee Red -- NRHA Hall of Fame; NRHA $12 Million Sire -- the first NRHA $12 Million Sire in history; deceased December 2009; NRHA Lazy E Classic Open Champion 1991; 3rd NRHA Open Futurity 1990; sired 1,543 registered Quarter Horses including seven NRHA Open Futurity Champions, four NRHA Open Derby Champions, three NRHA Million Dollar Sires -- including Conquistador Whiz -- and two NRHA Two Million Dollar Sires; the Topsail Whiz line is represented throughout this database including Walla Walla Whiz osw002, Whizkey N Diamonds osw037, and Spooks Gotta Whiz osw003) out of Sugarita Chex (Bueno Chex x -- Bueno Chex is a leading Quarter Horse sire whose daughters and granddaughters have contributed to multiple NRHA sire lines; dam offspring earnings in excess of $100,000 confirmed Silver Spurs Equine; Sugarita Chex produced Conquistador Whiz as her career-defining offspring; the Topsail Whiz x Bueno Chex dam cross paired the leading reining sire of his era with a proven producing dam whose sons and daughters include NRHA money earners across multiple disciplines). Owned by Silver Spurs Equine LLC. Silver Spurs Equine LLC, 14445 East Quail Track Road, Scottsdale AZ 85262. (480) 563-1966.

Cooled and frozen semen available. Stud fee $3,000 (special discount breedings available). Silver Spurs Breeders Futurity enrolled. Source: https://www.silverspursequine.com/conquistador-whiz-1 LTE $100,000+ confirmed NRHA.

PE $2,772,000 confirmed NRHA. NRHA Top-20 All-Time Leading Sire. NRHA Million Dollar Sire. The first-ever third-generation NRHA Million Dollar Sire -- grandsire Topsail Cody became a Million Dollar Sire in 1998, sire Topsail Whiz in 2001, and Conquistador Whiz joined the list making three consecutive generations of Million Dollar Sires.

Confirmed career: 2000 NRHA Open Derby Champion; 2000 NRBC Open Derby Champion; 2000 AQHA Congress Junior Reining Champion; 2000 Equi-Stat #1 Leading 4/5-Year-Old Reining Money-Earner; 1999 Congress Open Reining Champion; 2002 USET Festival of Champions USEF Reining Champion; 2002 FEI World Equestrian Games Spain Team Gold Medalist; 2002 World Equestrian Games Spain Individual Silver Medalist; 2002 World Equestrian Games Highest Individual Score. Winning both the 2000 NRHA Derby and the 2000 NRBC in the same season confirms Conquistador Whiz was the best open reining horse of his generation in that competition year. The 2002 World Equestrian Games Gold Medal team performance and Individual Silver Medal under the USET banner established him as an elite international competitor representing American reining on the world stage at the highest diplomatic level of the sport. His WEG Individual Silver Medal -- scored with the highest individual score at the entire Games -- is one of the most prestigious credentials in NRHA history.

Top offspring include KR Lil Conquistador (LTE $460,466; NRHA Open Futurity Champion; NRHA Open Derby Champion; NRBC Intermediate Open Derby Co-Champion); Quistador (LTE $234,293; 3rd NRHA Open Futurity; Tulsa Reining Classic Open Futurity Champion); Skeets Conquistador (LTE $124,094); JD Conquista Anna (LTE $56,429; 2006 NRHA Non-Pro Futurity Champion); Litn Up Conquistador (NRHA Non-Pro Futurity Champion ridden by Tish Fappani). The first-ever third-generation NRHA Million Dollar Sire status confirms that the Topsail Cody / Topsail Whiz / Conquistador Whiz lineage is among the most remarkable three-generation sire chains in NRHA history, producing champions at the highest level of competition across three consecutive breeding generations.
