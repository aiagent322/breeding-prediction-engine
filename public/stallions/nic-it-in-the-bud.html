Nic It In The Bud -- 1997 Stallion. AQHA. 14.2H. NRCHA Million Dollar Sire.

NRHA Million Dollar Sire. By Reminic (Docs Remedy x Fillinic -- NRCHA #5 All-Time Leading Sire; NRHA #9 All-Time Leading Sire; NCHA LTE $92,650; PE $4.2M+ with producers whose get exceeds $11M; Fillinic his dam is NRCHA Hall of Fame and one of the greatest performance horses on the West Coast; Reminic is the sire of Boomernic ss003 in this database -- the founding Silver Spurs stallion who won the 1992 NRHA Open Futurity and was inducted into the NRHA Hall of Fame; Nic It In The Bud and Boomernic are both Reminic sons making them full brothers in blood on the top side) out of Genuine Redbud (Genuine Doc x -- Genuine Doc is an NRHA Hall of Fame inductee and an NRHA All-Time Leading Sire; the Reminic x Genuine Doc dam cross brings together the West Coast Reminic line and the foundational Genuine Doc NRHA performance genetics; the Genuine Doc connection through Genuine Redbud gives Nic It In The Bud a versatile cross-discipline genetic foundation that has expressed itself across NRHA, NRCHA, and AQHA competition throughout his offspring). Owned by Silver Spurs Equine LLC. Scottsdale, Arizona.

(480) 563-1966. Cooled and frozen semen available. Stud fee $3,500. Silver Spurs Breeders Futurity enrolled.

AQHA ROM earning 32 Reining, 6 Cutting, and 8 Working Cow Horse points. Trained and shown throughout career by NRCHA Million Dollar Rider Todd Bergen. Confirmed career: 2000 NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Finals qualifier; 2000 NRHA Open Futurity Open Finals qualifier; 2001 NRCHA Stakes Open Champion; 2001 NRCHA Derby Open Champion; 2001 AQHA World Championship Show Junior Reining Finalist; 2003 World's Greatest Horseman Reserve Champion (bridle horse); 2003 NRCHA Futurity Open Bridle Sweepstakes Reserve Champion; NRCHA top 10 leading sire 2011; NRHA top 10 leading sire 2011. Qualifying for and making the Open finals at both the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity and the NRHA Open Futurity in the same season as a three-year-old is a credential earned by very few horses in history.

These two events -- the premier NRCHA annual event in Reno and the premier NRHA annual event in Oklahoma City -- represent opposite ends of the performance horse spectrum, separated by less than three months. Making the Open finals at both in the same year confirms that Nic It In The Bud was genuinely elite in both cow horse and reining simultaneously -- not merely competitive in one with token participation in the other. His 2001 NRCHA Stakes Open Championship and Derby Open Championship swept in a single season as a four-year-old is his career-defining achievement. Winning both the Stakes and the Derby Open in the same year under Todd Bergen's saddle confirms that Nic It In The Bud was the best NRCHA open competitor of his generation in 2001.

The 2003 World's Greatest Horseman Reserve Championship as a six-year-old bridle horse confirms that his talent extended into the full progression of the NRCHA horsemanship disciplines from snaffle bit through two-rein and into the bridle. As a NRCHA top-10 and NRHA top-10 leading sire simultaneously in 2011, he is one of only a handful of stallions in history to hold simultaneous top-10 rankings in both major western performance associations in the same year.
