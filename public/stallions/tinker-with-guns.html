Full Profile

Tinker With Guns -- 2006 Sorrel Stallion. AQHA. 14H. By Colonels Smoking Gun (Colonelfourfreckle x Katie Gun -- NRHA Hall of Fame; NRHA $15 Million Sire -- the only NRHA $15 Million Sire in history; sire of Gunnatrashya osw001, Down Right Amazing osw013, HF Mobster ss008, and numerous other champions throughout this database; the Colonels Smoking Gun line defines the modern NRHA reining era) out of Tinker Nic (Reminic x -- Reminic is NRHA #9 All-Time Leading Sire and NRCHA #5 All-Time Leading Sire, sire of Boomernic ss003 in this database; Tinker Nic is a daughter of Reminic whose produce record confirmed Tinker With Guns as her career-defining offspring; the Colonels Smoking Gun x Reminic dam cross through Tinker Nic pairs the NRHA $15 Million Sire with the Reminic Hall of Fame line in an unusual combination that creates offspring with both the Gunner competitive fire and the Reminic West Coast cow sense; Tinker Nic's Reminic foundation adds depth to the Colonels Smoking Gun top side and creates genetic diversity from the stallions at Silver Spurs who are primarily bred from Topsail Whiz, Shining Spark, or Smart Chic Olena dam lines).

Owned by Silver Spurs Equine LLC. Purcell, Oklahoma. Cooled and frozen semen available. Stud fee $2,000.

Silver Spurs Breeders Futurity enrolled. Source: https://www.silverspursequine.com/tinker-with-guns LTE $346,366 confirmed NRHA. PE $1,521,000+ confirmed. NRHA Two Million Dollar Sire.

Confirmed career: 2011 NRHA Open Derby Champion; 2012 NRHA Open Derby Co-Reserve Champion (tie); Top 5 NRHA Open Futurity 2009. Consecutive NRHA Derby performances at the open championship level in 2011 and 2012 confirm Tinker With Guns as one of the most sustained open competitors of his generation. Winning the 2011 NRHA Open Derby Championship and returning the following season to tie for the 2012 NRHA Open Derby Co-Reserve Championship is an exceptional consecutive performance. The NRHA Open Derby is one of the three most prestigious annual NRHA premier events, and very few horses have ever held a top-five finish in the Open division in two consecutive Derby seasons.

Tinker With Guns' two-year Derby record confirms that his talent and physical soundness carried through his full competitive career with the consistency that defines truly elite individuals rather than one-year performers. His pe of $1,521,000+ with offspring competing across NRHA and ranch riding events confirms that the Colonels Smoking Gun x Reminic dam combination consistently transmits competitive ability to the next generation. Top offspring include Tinker With Dreams (osw035 in this database -- standing at Oswood Stallion Station with LTE $432,285; 2021 NRHA Derby Open Champion -- confirming that the Tinker With Guns sire line has now produced a second-generation NRHA Derby Champion through his son). The fact that Tinker With Dreams osw035 stands at Oswood Stallion Station and has himself won the NRHA Derby confirms that Tinker With Guns produced a son whose own arena and breeding credentials equal and potentially exceed his own -- one of the clearest signs of a reliable sire line.

Breeders crossing Wimpys Little Step daughters or Shining Spark daughters on Tinker With Guns access the Colonels Smoking Gun x Reminic combination at a $2,000 stud fee at Silver Spurs Oklahoma -- one of the most accessible entry points to the proven Gunner sire line through a son with a confirmed NRHA Derby Championship and a two-year Open Derby career record that stands among the best in Silver Spurs history.
